Your Majesty,

My name is Mr. Candelex.

This morning I received a notice that my brother will be executed on the gallows for the murder of his wife.

I would like to ask you to reconsider your decision, because he is only a victim of his deteriorating mental health.

From a very young age, our father hit us. This caused my brother to prefer staying alone or “talking” with animals. All of this left him with a silent trauma that he carried for many years.

After he left home and got married, he sent me letters telling me how happy he was.

He had finally found someone who loved animals as much as he did.

He also told me that I was going to be an uncle.

I was very happy for my little brother. After all those years, it seemed like he was finally happy.

Months passed, but I didn't receive any more letters.

I didn't want to bother him, but I couldn't hold back anymore. I wanted to know what my nephew was going to be like.

Then, one day, I received a terrible letter.

Their firstborn had not survived childbirth.

I was crying when I read the letter.

He continued writing that he was doing very badly. He couldn't stop drinking to forget what had happened, and his relationship with his wife was getting worse and worse. There was no one he could talk to about how he was feeling.

Little by little, this way of escaping from his pain turned into a dependency that he could no longer break free from on his own.

I immediately wrote him a letter, telling him that I would arrive in the village within a few months.

So yes, my brother became an alcoholic to escape the difficult situation he was living through, and the alcohol only made his new and old traumas come out.

I am not going to deny that my brother is guilty of the murder of his wife, nor do I pretend to justify his actions. Her death was a tragedy, and my brother is responsible for his actions. But I also believe it was the terrible outcome of a situation that had been deteriorating for years. It was a bomb waiting to go off.

Now we all see him as the monster of the story, but how many people offered him help? How many even asked him how he was feeling?

What good will the gallows do? Will it bring his wife back to life? Will it erase the traumas that drove him to this point? Will it prevent another person suffering in the same way from ending up in a similar situation?

The execution will be quick, but it will solve nothing about what caused this tragedy. If we really want justice, we should first try to understand what happened and give him the opportunity to receive the help he never had.

For this reason, I ask you to send my brother to a special place where he can receive help and recover.

If this letter was not enough, I shall arrive in the village very soon with a great fortune, and we will be able to discuss my brother's condition more thoroughly.

Thank you very much.

Mr. Candelex
